# Run 0101: Story steps plateau and compare

Wave 015 (`editor-reshape-and-loose-ends`), item W-13 (phase P1). Focus: a step of a story can be told at a plateau and compared with another one (#24, second half)

Document profile: `single-document`, as runs 0089 to 0100. The design is settled by the author's decisions of 2026-10-18 on #100 (b) and #24 ("a step that carries a plateau and a comparison is the same feature as saving a comparison in a story"), and by the plan row of W-13; what is left to decide is a handful of rules, each of which fits in a line below and is pinned by a shared case. The review passes (a read of this document against the code, the open questions, the C scan) are answered below.

## Scope

In: `Story.Step` (two keys), `LayerLoader` and `WorkspaceFactory` (the check of the plateaus a step names), the editor (`standalone.html`: the check, `stepProblem` and `viewProblem` in the plateau of the step, `goToStoryStep`, the story bar, the story editor, *Keep as named view*), the shared cases (`layer-checks-cases.json` K37 to K40, `negative-cases.json` N83 to N85), the shipped example (`domain_plateaus`, a story), the tests, `CLAUDE.md`.

Out: the matrix of N plateaus (#24, first half, re-aimed), the manual and the tutorial (W-18: what they must say is in Findings), the command line drawing a story (issue #109).

## Decisions

- **D1. The keys are `at` and `against`**, as the plan chose (T2 of the plan, `wav-015` Open Questions): `at` is the plateau the step is told at, `against` the plateau it is compared with. Neither is the `plateau:` of a header (it means "belongs to"). Nothing found in the plan or in the issues says otherwise (#100's challenge proposes `draw` or `at`), so they stay.
- **D2. Defaults.** No `at`: the plateau the reader has drawn. No `against`: no comparison, except when the step names neither key, which is drawn as the reader had it (plateau and comparison). An empty text (`at: ""`, `against:` with nothing) names nothing, in both tools (a key with no value is absent, run 0092).
- **D3. The rule for `against` without `at`.** It is allowed: the step is told at the plateau drawn and compared with `against` ("what changes from `baseline` to where I am"). It is the reading the issue's wording gives (both optional, default the plateau drawn). When `against` is the plateau drawn there is no comparison (a plateau is never compared with itself, the editor's own rule), and the step is no mistake. When both keys are written and are one plateau, it is a mistake the file can be repaired for: one sentence.
- **D4. The checks, one sentence each, the same in both tools.** `at` and `against` must be a plateau of the workspace (``the story `s` (`file`) step 2 says `at: x`, which is not a plateau of this workspace (the plateaus are `a`, `b`)``, and for a workspace that has no plateau ``... but the workspace declares no plateau (a `plateau` key in a header, ...)``, as `settings.current` says it); and ``... step 3 says `at: p` and `against: p`: a plateau is never compared with itself``. They are told with the plateau-free check, after the layers, for each step before the names the step carries (`LayerLoader.stepPlateauProblems`; `stepPlateauProblems` in the page), for the stories of the manifests and those of `workspace.yaml`. The command line refuses a workspace that has no plateau too (`WorkspaceFactory.requireNoPlateauInSteps`): the cases of the shared files are all layered, so that case is in `StepPlateausTest` and `story-steps-plateaus.test.js`. The page lists them in the dialog of the mistakes in a section of their own (*The steps of the stories name plateaus that are none*), whatever the workspace; the step says it in the story too.
- **D5. A name the plateau of the step lacks is a state of the step, never a mistake.** The page looks at the view of a step in the plateau of the step (`viewProblem(view, plateau)`, `indexOfPlateau`) and says ``The element “x” is not in the plateau “p”.`` (the sentence of `viewLacksInPlateau`, with the plateau of the step), the model of a plateau being the one `layerModelOf` keeps. A plateau that the kernel refuses cannot be drawn: the step says so. The command line does not say it: it draws no story (D6), and telling it would take the load of a second plateau (what D4 of run 0100 avoided on purpose). N84 pins that the command line says nothing.
- **D6. The command line reads and checks; it does not draw stories.** The plan says so ("the command line reads them and refuses an unknown plateau with one sentence"), the manual says the command line ignores the stories, and a story drawn by the command line would need decisions that are not in any issue (folder, file names, `map` viewpoint, what is said for a plateau that lacks a name). The brief of the run asked for `views generate` to draw the step; it is filed as issue #109 with those questions, and `views generate` is byte for byte what it was (the identity check below).
- **D7. Playing a step (editor).** `goToStoryStep` draws the plateau and the comparison of the step (`drawPlateausOfStep`: `drawPlateau(id, { compare: 'other:<against>', fromAddress: true })`, so that the band, the Changes panel and the diagram are the ones of the plateau, and the step, not the plateau, is the entry of the history), then looks at the view. A step that names no plateau is drawn as the reader had it when the story began (`storyHome`): the plateau and the comparison on screen at the first step, so that the order in which the steps are visited does not change what a step draws. A plateau or a comparison the reader chooses on the band in the middle of the story is the new home (`storyHome.set` tells what the story drew last). *Leave story* changes nothing, as its tooltip says (the diagram stays as it is). A step with a plateau that is none, or that cannot be drawn, changes nothing: the bar says why and stays. The bar says *told at X, compared with Y*, and *Back to this step* is offered when the plateau or the comparison on screen is not the step's (`stepPlateausMatchScreen`).
- **D8. Writing (editor).** The story editor has two lists per step, *Told at* (default *The plateau drawn*) and *Compared with* (default *Nothing*), in a workspace that has plateaus or when the step already names one (a plateau that is none is listed, flagged *(not a plateau)*, so that it is not lost). `setStepPlateau` changes the step in memory and calls `markEntryChanged` (the manifest is rewritten line by line, the key is added at the end of the step and a choice that is the default takes it out; put back, the file is the one on disk). Nothing in the models changes, so there is no `modelsChanged()` to call (a story is no part of the elements; the check and the models of plateaus are cached on the text of the manifests, which changes). *Keep as named view* keeps the two keys (a step that points at a view keeps them: they are the step's, not the view's). *Add to story...* writes none: a story that tells the same thing whatever plateau is drawn stays the default; issue #110.
- **D9. No new class to register.** `Story.Step` is already in `reflect-config.json` (with its builder); the new code is static methods and functions.
