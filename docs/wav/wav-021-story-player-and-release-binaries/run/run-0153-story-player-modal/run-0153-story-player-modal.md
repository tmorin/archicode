# Run 0153: story-player-modal

Wave 021, W-2. *Play* opens a modal that covers the window and plays the story like slides, decoupled from the editor (GitHub issue tmorin/archicode#153; the commit of the wave closes it, this run does not). It builds on W-1 (run 0152): the scene of a step (`storyStepScene`), the live region `#story-announcer`, the menu of the steps. No Java, no Rust, no manual change (the manual is W-6's); the command line is not touched (T1).

Profile `single-document`: one page, one modal, one address and their tests; the plan decisions T3, T5 and T6 hold the design, the author's words of the brief are the requirements ("when I click Play a modal appears and I can play the story as with slides; the modal takes all the available space"). The review passes are answered below.

## Decisions

| # | decision | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | where the modal lives | `#story-player`, a container beside `#app` and `#story-announcer` in the static HTML (hidden until opened), filled by `renderStoryPlayerHtml()`. `render()` replaces `#app` and never touches it, so the player survives anything that redraws the editor; the editor behind is `inert` while it is open (`#app.setAttribute('inert')`), the focus trap (Tab round the player's controls) covers the ends of the list and a focus that is outside. `position: fixed; inset: 0; height: 100dvh`, one flex column (bar, plateaus, stage, status, words) with `min-width: 0` and `overflow: hidden`, the bar wraps, the words scroll in their own box. |
| 2 | state | `storyPlayer` (view state, not in `AppState`): `{ story, step, home, scenes, scene, seq, framing, keyOpen, notice, opener, entry }`. `home` is `storyHomeNow()` taken at *Play* and never moved; `scenes` keeps the scene of each step seen (a revisit is not computed again); `seq` drops the answer of a step the reader has left; `framing` is the player's own zoom and pan. |
| 3 | the scene and the toggles | each step is `storyStepScene(story, i, { home, readonly: true })`. W-1's finding is fixed: the scene read `AppState.changesOnly` and `AppState.hideWording` at call time; it now takes `changesOnly` and `hideWording` as options, off unless given (`togglesOf`; `drawingOfPlateaus(plateau, against, toggles)`), so the editor's choices are never read. The editing walk is untouched (its own `editorDrawing()` reads them). Two helpers were factored out of `storyStepScene` so that the menu looks at a step in the plateau it is drawn at: `stepDrawnAt(step, home)` and `stepPlateauAt(step, home)`. |
| 4 | Play, and what leaves the bar (T3) | the Stories page's *Play* is `data-action="story-player-open"` (it was `story-play`, which still draws a step on the editing diagrams: it is the per-step *Show on diagram*). The bar over the editing diagram loses *Previous*, *Next* and the menu (`story-prev`, `story-next`, `chooseStoryStep`, their entries in the click table and in `ACTIONS_LEAVING_THE_FORM`, the `change` branch of `handleAppChange`, the `.story-step-menu` rule); it keeps title, "Step n of N", the plateaus, *Back to this step*, *Edit text*, *Edit story*, *Leave story*. No test of the bar needed one more control. The bar has no *Play* of its own (the brief said to look: only the Stories page has one, and T3 lists what the bar keeps). `story-player-open` does not clear `storyBarNotice` or `removedStep` (the editor behind is untouched) and is not an action that leaves the form (no confirmation: nothing is replaced). |
| 5 | the menu | `renderStoryStepMenuHtml(story, current, className, { home, label, title })` is the wave 020 function made the player's: a native `<select data-player-step-menu>`, a step `stepProblem` refuses (in its own plateau, from the player's home) marked "— cannot be shown: …". The one handler is `handleStoryPlayerChange` on the container; `chooseStoryStep` is gone (it was `goToStoryStep`, the editing walk). |
| 6 | the slide (T6) | title (`<h2>` that labels the dialog), "Step n of N · view", menu, *Previous*, *Next*, *Full screen* (only where `document.fullscreenEnabled`; the Fullscreen API on the container; a refusal or a request that fails sets a notice and the modal stays; `fullscreenchange` redraws the button; closing leaves it), *Close*; the plateau and the comparison the step is drawn at, said and not chosen, "(from the editor)" for what the step leaves to the home; the diagram with its own zoom and pan; status line, hint ("Part of the diagram is out of view: Fit shows all of it"), the lines key and the comparison key; the words (`white-space: pre-line`); the step announced through `#story-announcer`. A step that cannot be drawn: `.player-problem` with the reason and "Use Previous, Next or the menu to go on", the way on open, announced "— cannot be shown: …". A scene that fails (an exception) is the same, never a stuck modal. |
| 7 | the diagram | the scene's markup is put in a canvas with the editor's own classes (`.diagram-canvas`, `.diagram-tools`) and stripped by `playerDiagramHtml`: no `data-action`, no `role="link"` (`role="img"`), no `tabindex`, the buttons of the offline list become spans, so a click on a box does nothing and nothing says it is a link (no selection, no inspection). Zoom and pan are the pure parts of the editor's framing (`diagramFitScale`, `diagramAutoScale`, `diagramFrameCenter`, `DIAGRAM_SCALES`) over `storyPlayer.framing`: buttons (−, 100%, +, Center, Fit), the wheel (ctrl+wheel zooms around the pointer), a drag; a step opens framed afresh; a window resize frames again. No element is put in the middle (a slide has no selection): a drawing that does not fit is shown from its top. No keyboard pan: the arrows are the steps'. |
| 8 | the key of a comparison | the scene's key (`#diagram-key`, `toggle-diagram-key`) is the page's; `playerLegendHtml` gives it its own ids and button (`#player-key`, `data-player-action="toggle-key"`), folded until opened (`keyOpen`, view state of the player), so there is no duplicate id with the editor behind it and `diagramKeyOpen` is not touched. The status area caps the open key at 24 vh (`--dg-key-max`). |
| 9 | keys (T6) | `handleStoryPlayerKeydown`, a capture listener on the document: Escape closes (also from the menu: a native list that owns the arrows chooses with them, Escape alone leaves), Left and Right, Home and End walk the steps (no-ops at the ends, still taken), Tab goes round. The wave 020 rule: left alone with Ctrl, Alt, Meta or Shift, with a typing target or a control that owns the arrows (`isTypingTarget`, the target and the focused element), with a dialog, the help or the header menu open, and for the other keys. `handleChangesKeydown` (`j`, `k`) is off while the player is open. |
| 10 | the address (T5) | `play=<story>&step=n` is the last key of `currentRouteHash()` while the player is open. The bar's own `story=` and `step=` leave the address while it is open (one `step=`, so a link has no two); they are back with the editor's address when it closes. Opening is `pushState` (an entry; `entry: true`), choosing a step `replaceState`, so no entry; `handleHashChange` (from the `hashchange` listener, after the early return of an address that is the editor's own) closes the player when the address has no `play=` or another story (Back), opens it when the address has one (Forward, a typed address), shows another step for another `step=`, compares the editor's part without the two keys (`hashWithoutPlayer`), and writes the address back when it names nothing. Escape and *Close* go Back (`history.back()`) when the history holds the entry the player made, so that Forward opens it again; a player opened by a link at load has no entry (`replace`) and rewrites the address in place. A link at load: `loadWorkspace` reads `storyPlayerAddress()` before `render()` (which writes the address back without `play=`) and opens the player after it, for a served folder and a picked one alike; the home is the plateau of the address. Unknown story, a step that is none (missing, not a number, out of range), a story with no step and a page with no story leave it closed, the address written back as it really is. |
| 11 | focus (T6) | opens on *Next* (else *Previous*, *Close*); a redraw of the player keeps the focus on the control that had it (or the nearest that can take it: a disabled *Next* at the last step hands it to *Previous*); closing gives it to the control that opened it (`opener`, the Play that was clicked, whether or not the browser focuses a button on click), else the same action in `#app`, else the *Play* of the story. `resetUiState` closes the player with the folder. |
| 12 | what is not done | no *Play* on the bar; no keyboard pan; no prefetch of the next step (a scene is drawn when asked for, kept after); no scene cache across Play presses (a new Play starts from a new home); a touch pinch is not handled (the buttons zoom); the pane of the built-in browser never settles a Fullscreen request, so the grant path was tested in Node only. |

## Review passes (the trio's, answered here)

- **KDMLLC.** KISS: one container, one state object, one render function; the editor's drawing code is reused as is (the scene, the framing maths), nothing re-implemented. DRY: the menu, the key, the announcer, `isTypingTarget`, `storyStepTitle` are the wave 020/W-1 ones. MECE: what the player reads (the story, the home, the model of each step's plateau), writes (its own state, the address, the container) and never touches (everything of `AppState`, the editor's view state) are decisions 1 to 3 and 10. LEAN: no new dependency, no prefetch, no cache beyond `scenes`. LUCID: every new function has a doc comment saying the rule. CONSISTENT: the same words as the bar ("cannot be shown", "Step n of N"), the same key rule as `handleChangesKeydown`, the same classes as the editor's diagram.
- **Open questions.** Where does the player's `step=` go when the bar has its own: the bar's pair leaves the address while the player is open (one `step=`). Close by Back or by replace: Back when the history holds the entry. Opening focus: *Next*. Initial framing: a readable size, from the top (no focal element). Where Play is: the Stories page only (the bar has none; T3). The toggles: options of the scene, off.
- **C scan against the repository.** `render()`'s tail (`syncHashFromState`) keeps `play=` because `currentRouteHash()` carries it. `handleAppClick`'s first lines clear `storyBarNotice` and `removedStep`: `story-player-open` is exempt. `applyDiagramParamsFromHash` reads the first `step=`: while the player is open there is one. The tests that read the bar (`model-ops.test.js`, `story-steps-plateaus.test.js`, `story-player-scene.test.js`'s comparison with the tag) said Previous and Next: they say now that the bar has none; the comparison with `archicode-0.18.0` strips Previous/Next from the tag's markup and rewrites its Play, so a change to the editing layout is still caught.
- **Canonical impact.** `CLAUDE.md`: the bar's menu sentence of the stories paragraph is replaced by the player (one block: the modal, the state, the slide, the keys, the address, the tests). The manual is W-6's (below). No `dom` packet, no `arc` item.
