## Lessons Learnt

- A headless Firefox driven over WebDriver BiDi gave real screenshots when the built-in browser pane could not (hidden window): worth trying first for a field check. A process started in a snap-confined Firefox cannot be killed with kill or pkill; stopping its systemd --user scope works.
- A shared-host refactor (the diagram pipeline taking a recipe from its host) paid off: the three later runs and the field check found the Map and Elements untouched after every visit.
