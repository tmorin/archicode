# Editor: the band, the Changes panel, the stories and the Maps

What the editor shows: the band, the Changes panel and the story player (plateaus), and the Maps with their links, highlight, boxes per row and the address. The rules of the command line are in `src/main/java/io/morin/archicode/workspace/CLAUDE.md`.

## The band and drawing a plateau

- The band is on the pages of Timeline only and is read-only. It has two lists, _Plateau_ and _Compare with_ (`chooseOnBand`), both showing the tree of the plateaus: every plateau in the order of the tree, indented by its depth with em spaces (`BAND_INDENT`, not capped: one em space per level for a chain of any length; under 40rem on the page Plateaus half a rem per level, `.timeline-item` in the narrow block of `style.css`). The plateau drawn is disabled in _Compare with_. _Compare with_ has _Nothing_ and _Past_ (`past`, follows the plateau drawn) before the tree, so a plateau of the tree is a comparison chosen with `compareWithPlateau`, as `diff --plateau <drawn> --against <id>` reads it. A list chooses, it does not toggle: the plateau already compared with is not pressed twice. A plateau on top of the drawn one that the address or `toggleCompareWithFuture` follows is the selected entry of the tree; a `compare=future` with several is a disabled _Choose a future plateau_ entry selected with nothing selected in the tree. The addresses are `compare=past`, `future`, `future:<id>` and `other:<id>`. There is no other selector and no _Compare to_.
- One button of the band opens the mistakes of the plateaus drawn and compared with (`bandMistakes`). The tree and the counts are on the page Plateaus, and so are _New plateau_ and _Edit plateau_ (`plateauWritingHtml`). Functions: `bandIsShown`, `syncLayers`, `drawPlateau`, `toggleCompareWithPast`, `toggleCompareWithFuture`, `compareWithPlateau`.
- An address typed while the editor is open, or reached by Back and Forward, draws the plateau and the comparison it names (`syncPerspectiveFromHash` → `drawLayersNamedByAddress` → `drawPlateau(id, { compare, fromAddress })`; an address with no `plateau=` is the root, an unknown plateau leaves the one drawn, `compare=` is read when the address has it). A plateau chosen on the band or the page is an entry of the history (`layerChangeIsAnEntry`; `syncHashFromState` pushes instead of replacing).
- The Reading view is under the list of the Plateaus page (`presentation/plateau-matrix-page.js`, rule in `model/plateau-matrix.js` pinned by `matrix-cases.json`): _Read the path_ sets `plateauReading` (path and unfolded rows, in `ui-state.js`, cleared with the folder), a cell draws the pair as _Changes_ does (`openMatrixCell` → `drawPlateau`, then `goToChange`), and the address has `matrix=<id>` and `rows=all` (`currentRouteHash`, `syncPerspectiveFromHash`), dropped when the path cannot stand.
- Manual: `[[editor-band]]`, `[[editor-current-plateau]]`, `[[editor-rename]]`.

## The Changes panel and the key

- The Changes list is one function, `renderChangesPanelHtml`, written by the pages Plateaus, Applications, Environments, Elements and Stories. On Stories it is the Changes tab beside View on the page of a view, and alone on the right for a story, offered only while a comparison is on (`libraryRightHtml`). It has _Write replaces_ and _Not related_; _Everything / Changes only_ is among the tools of the diagram. _Write replaces_ writes the one entry the author chooses, in the manifest that declares the element, but only one its layer can hold (see `application/CLAUDE.md`); a comparison several layers apart takes its chain from `replacesChainBetween` over `layerModelOf`.
- The key under the diagram is `diagramComparisonLegendHtml` in `#diagram-legend` (`.diagram-status`). It is folded by default: one row with a sample of the box and of the link, the glyph, the word and the count of each state, and a _Key_ button (`toggle-diagram-key`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="diagram-key"`) that opens the sentence on how each line is drawn and the note about dangling references. The folded row wraps, and stays one row that scrolls along under 700 px. The open key is capped at 30 % of the diagram area (`--dg-key-max`, set by `syncDiagramKey`, which a `resize` of the window runs again) and scrolls inside. The choice is `diagramKeyOpen`, view state only: changed in place without a redraw, put back by `showDiagramScene`, not in the address, the workspace or `diagramCacheKey`, no `modelsChanged()`, folded again with a new folder (`resetUiState`); `diagram-key-folded.test.js`.
- The panel is read one change at a time: _Previous change_ and _Next change_, in the order of the list, stopping at the ends (`walkChange`, `currentChangeOf`, `changesWalk`). The keys `j` and `k` are the same walk: `handleChangesKeydown`, one listener on the document, leaves the key alone with a Ctrl, Alt, Meta or Shift, while the reader types (an input, a textarea, a select, a contenteditable, a CodeMirror editor, the target and the focused element both looked at), while a dialog, the help or the menu of the header is open or a rejected YAML text is waiting, and where no list is on screen; it gives no control the focus unless it was on the panel. Under the buttons `renderCurrentChangeDetailHtml` shows the "What changed" block of the change the walk is at, the one `renderWhatChangedHtml` writes for the inspection, so that Plateaus and a story with no view drawn show what a change is. A click on a row is unchanged: from Plateaus and from a story it opens Elements.
- The list is narrowed by a state and a layer (`changesFilter`, "12 of 167 changes", the selection moving to the nearest change shown: `setChangesFilter`). All of it is view state, not an input of the comparison nor of any diagram cache (no `modelsChanged()`), and the filters are all again when the pair of plateaus compared changes. Tests: `reading-changes.test.js` (on the 360 changes of `replaces-large-pair`), `changes-shortcut.test.js` (on the Brightcart pairs).
- The walk row is a sticky block under the `Selected | Changes` tabs: `.changes-walkbar` (`[data-changes-walkbar]`, a following sibling of `.changes-reading` under `.changes-panel`, `position: sticky`, `top: calc(-1rem + var(--right-tabs-h))`) holds _Previous change_, _Next change_, the position and _Show all_. `syncRightPanelSticky` (`presentation/right-panel.js`) sets `--right-tabs-h` and `--right-walk-h` on `#panel-right` after each render and on a resize, `.right-panel` takes their sum as `scroll-padding-top`, and `render()` brings the selected row into view with `block: 'nearest'`, so a walk moves the list by the least. The block is measured at 360 x 640 against a 35 % rule (the rows in their own scroll box would be built only above it). Safari is not proved: `changes-walk.spec.js` runs in Chromium and Firefox only. Manual: `[[editor-keyboard]]`.

## Stories and the story player

- The editor plays stories. `goToStoryStep` draws the plateau and the comparison of the step with `drawPlateau(..., { fromAddress: true })` before it looks at the view; a step that names none is drawn as the reader had it when the story began (`storyHome`, moved by a plateau the reader draws in the middle of the story; _Leave story_ changes nothing). `stepProblem` looks at the view in the plateau of the step (`viewProblem(view, plateau)`, `indexOfPlateau`) and says `The element “x” is not in the plateau “p”.`, the plateau that is none or cannot be drawn (`stepPlateauProblem`), and the comparison with itself. The bar over the diagram (_Show on diagram_, per step, on the page Stories) keeps what shows one step (title, "Step n of N", the words, _Edit text_, _Edit story_, _Leave story_, _Back to this step_) and has no _Previous_, _Next_ or menu: they are the player's.
- _Play_ opens the player: `data-action="story-player-open"` on the page Stories, disabled for a story with no step; `openStoryPlayer(storyId, index, { opener, address })` does nothing for a story or step that is none, a dialog open or a player already open. It is a modal that covers the window (`#story-player`, a container beside `#app` and `#story-announcer`, `position: fixed; inset: 0`, `.player-*` sheet, one column that never grows past the window: the bar wraps, the words scroll in their own box) and plays the story like slides, decoupled from the editor.
- `storyPlayer` is view state of the page (`{ story, step, home, scenes, scene, seq, framing, keyOpen, notice, opener, entry }`). `home` is `storyHomeNow()` taken when _Play_ was pressed and never moved. Each step comes from `storyStepScene(story, i, { home, readonly: true })` (the toggles `changesOnly` and `hideWording` are options of the scene, off unless given: the scene never reads the editor's; `stepDrawnAt`, `togglesOf`), kept by step in `storyPlayer.scenes`; the answer of a step the reader has left is dropped (`seq`).
- Nothing of the editor is read or written while the player is open: no `modelsChanged()`, no `AppState`, selection, perspective or file. `#app` is `inert` (the focus trap, Tab round the player's own controls, is for its ends), a redraw of the editor leaves the container alone, and closing puts nothing back because nothing moved (the focus goes to the _Play_ that opened it, `returnFocusAfterPlayer`; `resetUiState` closes it with the folder).
- The slide (`renderStoryPlayerHtml`) has: the title (the dialog's label, `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`); "Step n of N"; the menu of the steps (`renderStoryStepMenuHtml(story, current, className, { home, label, title })`, a native `<select data-player-step-menu>`, one entry per step, "n. title" from `storyStepTitle`; a step `stepProblem` refuses, looked at in its own plateau, is marked "— cannot be shown: <sentence>" and carries the sentence as its title; handled by `handleStoryPlayerChange`); _Previous_, _Next_, _Full screen_ (the Fullscreen API on the container, only where `document.fullscreenEnabled`; a refusal says so in `player.notice` and the modal stays; `fullscreenchange` redraws the button; closing leaves it) and _Close_; the plateau and the comparison the step is drawn at, said and not chosen ("(from the editor)" for what the step leaves to the home, `playerPlateausHtml`); the diagram with the player's own zoom and pan (`framing`, `applyPlayerFraming`, `setPlayerScale`, `movePlayerBy`: the pure parts of the editor's framing over the player's state, opened at a readable size with no element in the middle, with the hint "Part of the diagram is out of view" as the editor's; buttons, the wheel and a drag), its markup stripped of what selects (`playerDiagramHtml`: no `data-action`, no `role="link"`, no tab stop: no selection, no inspection); its status line and the key of a comparison with its own ids and button (`playerLegendHtml`, `toggle-key`); for a step that cannot be drawn its reason; and the words. The step is announced through `#story-announcer`, a live region outside `#app` (`storyStepAnnouncement`, `announceStoryStep`, `sayPlayerStep`, "— cannot be shown: …" for a problem; emptied on close).
- Keys (`handleStoryPlayerKeydown`, on the document, in the capture phase): Left and Right, Home and End walk the steps, Escape closes (from the menu too, a list that owns the arrows), Tab goes round. It follows the rule of `handleChangesKeydown` (a typing target or a control that owns the arrows, a modifier, a dialog, the help or the header menu open: the key is left alone); `j` and `k` of the changes are off while the player is open.
- The address is `play=<story>&step=n` after the editor's own keys; the bar's `story=` and `step=` leave the address while the player is open, so that there is one `step=` (`currentRouteHash`, `storyPlayerAddress`, `hashWithoutPlayer`, `handleHashChange`). Opening is an entry of the history (`pushState`), choosing a step replaces it, Back closes it, Forward opens it again at its step, Escape and _Close_ go Back when the history holds the entry (`player.entry`), else rewrite the address in place. A link opens the player at load once the folder is open (`loadWorkspace`, read before `render()`, which writes the address back without it; the home is the plateau of the link). An address that names no story, a step that is none, a story with no step or a page without stories leaves it closed and is written back as it really is.
- The scene of a step is drawn on the side, not in the editor: `storyStepScene(storyId, index, { home, readonly, changesOnly, hideWording })` (async) gives `{ storyId, index, count, title, text, announcement, plateau, against, told, problem, comparison, diagram }`. It takes the plateau and the comparison the step names (`stepDrawing` over a `home` that the caller fixes when _Play_ is pressed, `storyHomeNow()`), their models from `layerModelOf` (`drawingOfPlateaus`, which has the shape of `editorDrawing()`), the comparison from `comparisonStateOf(drawing, memo)` (the kernel's `compare`, kept on `layerCache.comparisons`; `comparisonState()` is the same function over the editor's drawing and `comparisonCache`), and the diagram from `computeDiagramScene(recipe, input, comparison, source, kernel, elk, viewKey, readonly)`, the function `mountDiagramIfNeeded` draws through as well (`source` is null for the editor's own model and `{ index, baselineIndex, comparison, changesOnly }` for a step; `buildKernelInput`, `diagramItemText`, `tellRemovedItems`, `buildDiagramGhosts` and `renderDiagramSvg` take it as an option; `viewDiagramRecipe(view, index, host)` is the recipe of a view in a model). It writes no `AppState`, page, address or file, keeps no cache but `layerCache`, reads no selection, and never calls `modelsChanged()`. A step that cannot be drawn has its `problem` (`stepProblem(step, plateau)`, looked at in the plateau of the step) and no diagram.
- Tests: `story-player-modal.test.js` (with `story-player-page.js`: the container, the focus and `inert` in Node) plays every step of the four shipped stories from several homes in the modal, step by step, by keys and from the menu, compares it box by box and link by link with the editing walk, and checks that the editor is byte for byte what it was after. `story-player-scene.test.js` draws every step the same way and compares the Stories page and the bar with the page of 0.18.0 (Previous, Next and Play apart; the menu is the player's).
- The story editor writes the keys `at` and `against` with two lists per step (`setStepPlateau`, through `markEntryChanged`: no model changes, so no `modelsChanged()`), and _Keep as named view_ keeps them. _Add to story..._ has a box in the story picker's dialog (`storyStepPlateaus`, `storyPlateauChoiceHtml`), offered only when the workspace has plateaus and CHECKED when a comparison is on screen: checked, the new step is written with `at` (the plateau drawn) and `against` (the plateau compared with, as the id it resolves to now: a following _Past_ is written as that plateau, and the dialog says so); unchecked, the step has no key. A comparison the page cannot name (the future still to choose, a plateau that is gone, the plateau drawn itself) leaves `against` out and says why. Plateaus that cannot be drawn offer no box, or a disabled one, so the keys written always pass `stepPlateauProblem` (`add-to-story-plateaus.test.js`, on the Brightcart story `order_journey`).
- Manual: `[[editor-stories]]`, `[[editor-story-player]]`, `[[editor-keyboard]]`, `[[editor-small-screen]]`.

## Maps: links, highlight, boxes per row

- The _Applications_ / _Relationships_ toggles of the technology Map. On the page Environments the tools of the diagram have the group _Applications_ / _Relationships_ (`renderMapPartsToolHtml`, action `toggle-map-part`, `aria-pressed`, no key), also on the page of a saved view of the technology Map, where they edit the view like the texts do (`setViewMapPart`; the form of the view has the boxes `part:applications` and `part:relationships`). The state is `technologyMapParts` (reset with the folder; `MAP_PARTS`, `mapPartsShown()`), kept in the address as `applications=0` and `relationships=0` (only what is left out), read back by `applyDiagramParamsFromHash`, and part of the key of the diagram cache (`diagramCacheKey`, for the technology Map only). It is view state and never model state (no `modelsChanged()`); a toggle asks the Map to drop a selection it no longer draws (`mapSelectionCheck`).
- Links. The page Applications lists the relationships under its tree (`presentation/map-links.js`; entries in `model/map-links.js`: source, destination and trimmed label, the kernel's rule), and ticking some is the `links` of the Map. `mapLinks` is view state, no `modelsChanged()`: it is in the diagram cache key, in the address as `rel=<JSON [source, destination, label?]>` once per entry (an entry no model has is dropped), restored by a saved view and written back into `properties.links` by the view form and _Keep as named view_. A view with a highlight never matches the screen. The panel of a selected link has a check box per relationship and _Draw only this link_; a chip over the diagram clears the choice. Rename and move rewrite `links` and `highlight` of views and steps (`rewriteSavedViewReferences` in `application/story-entries.js`; the label never changes). The technology Map has none. The load check of `links` in a layered workspace is not built.
- Highlight. The editor draws the highlight the kernel marks (`renderDiagramSvg` in `presentation/diagram-layout.js`: `dg-hl` on the heavy link, `dg-calm` on the others, the marker `dg-arrow-hl` only in a diagram with a highlighted link). The kernel's `highlighted` flag is the one source; the editor adds no match of its own. `mapHighlight` is view state beside `mapLinks` (`model/ui-state.js`: in the cache key and in the address as `hl=`; `snapshotWriteState` and `resetUiState` know it).
- Its controls: the _Highlight_ box of a link's panel and its chip (`presentation/map-links.js`), the _Highlight_ field of a saved view (`setViewHighlight`, `presentation/view-edit.js`) and of a story step (`setStepHighlight`, `application/story-actions.js`, which writes the step's own key; it replaces the view's, `stepViewForDrawing`). _Add to story..._ writes the live highlight as the step key with the links in `properties.links`; _Keep as named view_ writes it as `properties.highlight`; `canonicalViewProperties` writes `highlight` sorted, without duplicates, after `links`. The player draws each step with `storyStepScene`, which reads the step's highlight, and announces it (`presentation/story-scene.js`, `storyStepAnnouncement`) without writing to the editor.
- The shipped story `order_flow` (the layered Brightcart's `shared/story.order_flow.yaml`, also in the samples) is the written form of a story of a flow. `scripts/byte-identity-new-files.txt` lists the files that `stories generate` writes and that differ from the 0.19.0 jar by design; `scripts/byte-identity.sh` reads it.
- Tests: `map-links-*.test.js`, `map-highlight-*.test.js`, `story-player-highlight.test.js`, `map-links-shared.test.js` (with `src/test/resources/map-links/expected-boxes.json`, also read by `MapLinksSharedTest`), and the e2e specs `src/test/e2e/map-links.spec.js` and `story-flow.spec.js`. Manual: `[[editor-map-links]]`, `[[map-highlight]]`, `[[editor-stories]]`, `[[editor-story-player]]`, `[[story-of-a-flow]]`.
- Boxes per row. `layout: {columns: N}` (a whole number from 1 to 20), in `properties` of a view and in `settings.views.properties.<viewpoint>`, is read by the editor only; the command line reads neither. `resolveDiagramColumns({ toolbar, view, settings, viewpoint })` in `model/diagram-columns.js` is the one place that knows the four levels: the toolbar, then the view, then the workspace, then none; the first level that is set decides, and an invalid one gives `columns: null` and its sentence from `columnsProblem` (`viewpoint` is `'map'` for both Maps; `DIAGRAM_COLUMNS_MAX` and `DIAGRAM_COLUMNS_CHOICES` live there).
- `mainDiagramRecipe`, `viewDiagramRecipe` and `libraryDiagramRecipe` (`presentation/diagram-page.js`, `application/view-recipe.js`) carry `columns` and `layoutProblem`. `computeDiagramScene` returns the error panel for a `layoutProblem` before it lays anything out, and passes `columns` to `buildElkGraph` (`presentation/diagram-layout.js`), which sets `elk.layered.layering.strategy: COFFMAN_GRAHAM` and `coffmanGraham.layerBound` on the root and on each compound node, and adds nothing when `columns` is unset, so the graph is the one of before, key for key.
- `diagramColumns` is view state (`model/ui-state.js`, reset by `resetUiState`, not in `snapshotWriteState`); `diagramCacheKey` adds `cols:<n>` only when it is set; `applySavedView` takes the valid value of the view. A saved view keeps `layout` in its canonical form (`canonicalViewProperties` writes `{columns: N}`; `canonicalViewDefinition` drops the value the workspace gives; `VIEW_PROPERTY_ORDER` in `presentation/view-edit.js` puts it after `hide-unlinked` and before the texts; `setViewLayout` writes it; `currentViewDefinition` writes the toolbar's value only; `viewProblem` says an invalid one). The _Columns_ list is `renderColumnsToolHtml` (`presentation/diagram-controls.js`, `data-action="set-columns"`, handled by `chooseColumns` in `presentation/view-edit.js`, which does not call `pushDiagramHistory`); the form of a view has the same list (`presentation/stories-pages.js`, `layout:columns`). `readSettingsDraft` (`model/form-validation.js`) refuses an invalid `layout` for `overview`, `detailed` and `deep`; `map` is not in `VIEWPOINT_IDS`, so a Map's workspace value is checked when a Map is drawn (the sentence replaces the diagram).
- Tests: `diagram-columns.test.js`, `diagram-columns-elk.test.js` (the graph with no `columns` deep-equals a copy of the builder before the change, `diagram-columns-reference.js`) and `diagram-columns-view.test.js`. ELK does not run in the node suite, so the rows it draws are for the end-to-end suite to show. Manual: `[[editor-layout]]`, `[[editor-view-edit]]`, `[[editor-small-screen]]`, `[[technical-configuration]]`.

## The toolbar of a diagram

- `renderDiagramToolbarHtml(record, host)` (`presentation/diagram-page.js`) builds the toolbar, `div.diagram-toolbar[role=toolbar][aria-label="Diagram tools"]`, and `renderDiagramCanvasHtml` returns it, the stage and the status line as three siblings, so the toolbar is docked before the canvas on every surface (an element's three diagrams, both Maps, the page of a saved view, a story step drawn in the editor; the player has none). It holds five groups in this order, each built by `renderToolGroupHtml(name, html)` as `div.tool-group[role=group][aria-label=<Name>][data-group=<name>]` and left out when it has no control: Draw (folding, _Hide unlinked_, the parts of the technology Map, the Facet select; none on the page of a view), Texts (`renderDiagramShowGroupHtml`), Layout (_Columns_), Compare (the one _Changes only_ toggle, `data-action="set-changes-only"` with `data-value` the value a click sets, only while `layerCompareOther()`) and Frame (zoom, _Center_, _Fit_). Each control is in exactly one group.
- Draw, Texts and Layout carry `span.tool-group-dot` (`SAVED_GROUPS` in `presentation/diagram-toolbar.js`, equal to the groups of `toolbar-controls.json` that have a keyed control; Compare and Frame have no `key`). _Reset_ is the one control outside the groups: `button#diagram-reset-button[data-action="diagram-reset"]` after Layout, absent on the page of a saved view, `aria-disabled` (never `disabled`, so it keeps the focus) while `diagramDiffersFromOrigin()` is false. It calls `applyToolbarKeys(origin properties or {}, layer)`, the function `applySavedView` also uses for the keys of the toolbar, after `pushDiagramHistory()`, and never touches Changes only, zoom, pins, hidden, `links`, `highlight` or the selection. `diagramOrigin` is session state written by `applySavedView` only, read through `currentDiagramOrigin()`, which clears it when the page or the selected element no longer matches; nothing is written to a file or the address.
- Under 40rem every group but Compare is `div.tool-group-head` with `button.tool-group-button[data-action="toggle-tool-group"]` (name and `span.tool-group-count`, counted from the markup of the group's controls by `countOnControls`; Frame shows the zoom, updated by `applyDiagramScale`) and its controls are a panel (`#tool-panel-<group>`, `toolPanelOpen`, read through one function that clears it on a page or element change). `Escape` in the toolbar closes the panel before the other Escape branches of `handleDiagramKeydown`.
- A control is absent only when it can never apply: on the page of a saved view, Map views have _Fold all_, _Unfold all_ (`setViewOpened`) and every view has the Facet (`setViewFacet`), each one `editSavedView` call; `toolbar-controls.json` lists what is absent on a surface with its `why`.
- `src/test/javascript/viewpoint-kernel/toolbar-controls.json` is the table of the controls of each surface by group, and `toolbar.test.js` checks every surface against it: a new control is added to the table and to one group, never beside the toolbar. Every toggle (the toolbar's, the header _Maximize_, the story bar _Edit text_, the player's _Full screen_ and _Center_) says its state with `aria-pressed` and keeps a label that does not change; no other attribute says "on". Manual: `[[editor-layout]]`, `[[editor-texts]]`, `[[editor-changes-only]]`.

## The address of a diagram

- The address of a diagram page is the whole say of the diagram. An option is in the address when it changes which boxes and links are drawn, or where; never when it changes only the surroundings. `currentRouteHash` (`composition/address-routing.js`) writes it on the page of an element (diagram tab), on both Maps and on the page of a selected saved view.
- The keys: `layout=N` (the toolbar's `diagramColumns`, 1 to 20, for an element and the Maps; never the view's or the workspace's value, which are files; nothing on the page of a saved view, whose toolbar edits the file), `compare=<choice>` (every diagram surface of a layered workspace, empty for none; a workspace with no plateau writes none), `changes-only=1` (only with a comparison; never `=0`), `open=` and `hide=` (Maps; sorted lists of references like `pin`), after `facet`, `pin`, `rel`, `hl`, then `compare`, `changes-only` and `plateau`.
- Absent means: no layout, folded, none hidden, and `changes-only` off when `compare=` is present (else the flag is left as it is, so an older link without `compare=` changes nothing). The reader resets what the address does not say on a diagram page and never calls `rememberFlag` for `changes-only`, so the reader's remembered flag survives; `drawPlateau` does remember an addressed comparison (`rememberLayerChoice`).
- Never in the address: the wording-only toggle, the open or folded key, zoom and pan, the selection of a box. Tests: `diagram-address.test.js`, `diagram-address-audit.test.js` (holds each input of the drawing to a parameter or a reason) and `src/test/e2e/diagram-address.spec.js`. Manual: `[[editor-link-carries]]`.
